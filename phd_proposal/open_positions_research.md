# Global PhD Positions and Research Groups in Quantitative Finance and Machine Learning

This document outlines the top-tier universities, research institutes, and key academic contacts for doctoral studies at the intersection of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Empirical Asset Pricing.

---

## 1. University of Oxford
### **Oxford-Man Institute of Quantitative Finance (OMI)**
*   **Overview**: The premier interdisciplinary hub for machine learning in finance. OMI combines research from the departments of Mathematics, Statistics, Engineering Science, and Saïd Business School.
*   **Core Research Areas**:
    *   Natural Language Processing (NLP) for alternative data.
    *   Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) for market-making and portfolio execution.
    *   Deep learning for limit order book (LOB) modeling.
*   **Key Professors**:
    *   **Prof. Stephen Roberts** (Machine Learning, Engineering Science) - Bayesian ML, signal processing, time series.
    *   **Prof. Stefan Zohren** (Associate Professor, Engineering Science) - Lead researcher at OMI, specializing in deep learning, recurrent neural networks, and reinforcement learning for high-frequency trading.
*   **How to Apply**: You do not apply directly to OMI. You must apply for a PhD (DPhil) in Mathematics, Statistics, or Engineering Science, and then affiliate with OMI. Applications typically open in September and close in January.
*   **Funding**: OMI Scholarships, G-Research Doctoral Fellowships, and EPSRC doctoral training partnerships.

## 2. ETH Zurich
### **Department of Mathematics / ETH AI Center**
*   **Overview**: Renowned for theoretical and mathematical rigor, ETH Zurich is at the forefront of combining machine learning with mathematical finance (e.g., Deep Hedging).
*   **Core Research Areas**:
    *   **Deep Hedging**: Using neural networks to hedge derivatives under transaction costs and market frictions.
    *   Stochastic optimal control, reinforcement learning for financial agents.
    *   Mathematical foundations of deep neural networks.
*   **Key Professors**:
    *   **Prof. Josef Teichmann** (Mathematical Finance) - Lead pioneer of Machine Learning in mathematical finance and continuous-time deep learning.
    *   **Prof. Johannes Muhle-Karbe** (Mathematical Finance) - Portfolio theory, transaction costs, and market frictions.
*   **How to Apply**:
    1.  **Standard Doctorate**: Apply directly to a vacancy advertised by a specific professor in the Department of Mathematics.
    2.  **ETH AI Center Doctoral Fellowship**: A highly competitive program that requires dual supervision (e.g., one supervisor in Finance/Math and another in core AI/CS). Applications open annually in October.
*   **Funding**: Fully funded positions via Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) or ETH AI Center fellowships.

## 3. Imperial College London
### **CFM-Imperial Institute of Quantitative Finance**
*   **Overview**: Located in London, the institute is heavily connected with the city's top quantitative funds and market practitioners. It was created in partnership with Capital Fund Management (CFM).
*   **Core Research Areas**:
    *   High-frequency limit order books and market microstructure.
    *   Statistical mechanics of financial markets.
    *   Deep learning for risk management.
*   **Key Professors**:
    *   **Prof. Jack Jacquier** (Mathematics) - Mathematical finance, volatility modeling, deep learning.
    *   **Prof. Rama Cont** (Mathematics) - Mathematical modeling of systemic risk, market microstructure, and deep learning in finance.
*   **How to Apply**: Apply to the PhD in Mathematics or the PhD in Finance at the Imperial College Business School. The CFM-Imperial Institute offers research desks and collaborative supervision.
*   **Funding**: CFM-Imperial Doctoral Scholarships, EPSRC CDT in Mathematics of Random Systems.

## 4. Technical University of Munich (TUM)
### **TUM School of Management - Chair of Digital Finance**
*   **Overview**: A rising European powerhouse for empirical finance, FinTech, and ML-driven asset pricing.
*   **Core Research Areas**:
    *   Empirical asset pricing using machine learning and deep learning.
    *   Behavioral finance and sentiment extraction from financial media.
    *   Cryptocurrency markets and decentralized finance (DeFi).
*   **Key Professor**:
    *   **Prof. Dr. Florian Weigert** (Chair of Digital Finance) - Focuses on empirical asset pricing, mutual funds, and machine learning in equity markets.
*   **How to Apply**: TUM operates under the chair system. Doctoral positions (usually 75% TV-L E13 research assistant contracts) are advertised directly on the [TUM Chair of Digital Finance vacancy page](https://www.finance.mgt.tum.de/digital-finance/vacancies/).
*   **Funding**: Fully funded via research assistant contracts (German TV-L E13 scale).

## 5. Other Notable Programs
*   **Goethe University Frankfurt & SAFE**: The Graduate School of Economics, Finance, and Management (GSEFM). Extremely strong in econometric modeling, regularized regression, and structural estimation.
*   **Princeton University (Bendheim Center for Finance)**: Focuses on high-dimensional financial econometrics, machine learning, and macro-financial risk. Leading contact: **Prof. Markus Brunnermeier**.
*   **University of Chicago Booth School**: Pioneer of asset pricing theory. Strong focus on neural network-based factor models (e.g., Prof. Bryan Kelly, who is also Head of ML at AQR Capital Management).
